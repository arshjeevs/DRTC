\chapter{Conclusion and Future Work}\label{ch:conclusion}

\section{Conclusion}

We built a complete platform for remote inference of robot policies on a low-cost SO-101 arm, on top of LeRobot~0.7:
three recorded and trimmed datasets, seven policies trained with their authors' recipes, our implementation of
Distributed Real-Time Chunking with four fixes for slow links and short chunks, six smoothing methods, injection of
seven kinds of network fault, and an evaluation framework that freezes every plan, runs trials in seeded rounds, keeps
the raw record of every trial and rebuilds every number from it.

In the first robot run, a mix of a latency spike, dropped replies and a short outage lowered the task success of
LeRobot's shipped client on the sorting task from \R{main/smolvla/baseline/M1} and \R{main/groot/baseline/M1} to
\R{main/smolvla/faults/M1} and \R{main/groot/faults/M1}; the client itself ended every faulted trial at the first fault.
With \drtc{} under the same faults success was \R{main/smolvla/drtc/M1} and \R{main/groot/drtc/M1}, no trial was ended by
a fault, and the arm moved again within about a second and a half of every fault. Smoothing reduced the jerk by factors
of \R{main/smolvla/jerkratio} and \R{main/groot/jerkratio} without lowering success. Because the shipped client gives up
by design, the experiments were redesigned (setup~v2) to compare \drtc{} with a client that waits, to take \drtc{} and
the smoothing apart, and to cover two tasks and five policies on the bench; its results are reported in
Sections~\ref{sec:v2bench} and~\ref{sec:v2robot} as they arrive.

\section{Future work}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Complete setup~v2} and the baselines, and grow the robot plans to twenty trials where differences are
      close.
\item \textbf{More policies and tasks}: \pizerofive{} and MolmoAct~2 when trained, and the robot DRTC experiment on
      cube in box.
\item \textbf{The edge}: official runs with the Raspberry Pi~5 as the robot computer (Chapter~\ref{ch:edge}).
\item \textbf{Compression} of the camera images to shorten the round trip.
\item \textbf{RTC for relative-action policies} such as GR00T~N1.7, which needs the previous chunk's actions re-anchored
      to the new state.
\item \textbf{An edge fallback}: a small policy (ACT) on the robot computer that keeps the arm moving while the server is
      unreachable.
\end{itemize}
