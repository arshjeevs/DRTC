\chapter{Training Recipes}\label{app:recipes}

Every policy is trained with its authors' published recipe; Table~\ref{tab:recipesfull} gives the main settings. The full recipe
of each, with every flag, is in the project's registry (\texttt{lab/recipes/}).

\paragraph{Speed settings.} Settings that change only the speed of training are kept apart from the recipes: \smolvla{}
is compiled, data-loading workers follow each machine's CPU budget, and GR00T~N1.5 can read a cache of its pre-resized
frames. None of them changes what is learnt.

\paragraph{Served checkpoint.} Every run evaluates the held-out loss about 20 times; the Hub repository's root holds the
checkpoint with the lowest held-out loss, which is served to the robot, and the last checkpoint is kept with its
training state. The offline evaluation reports the action error of both (Section~\ref{sec:offline}).
