\section{Why it was redesigned}\label{sec:redesign}

Every trial of condition~2 was ended by upstream's own one-second starvation grace at the first fault, the latency
spike (Section~\ref{sec:main}). Three things followed.
\begin{itemize}
\item The faulted baseline gave up by design, and raising its limit is a single setting. Beating it shows that the
      default is fragile, not that \drtc's scheduling is better.
\item Condition~3 differed from condition~2 in two things at once: the scheduling and the patience (a whole-trial
      grace). The recovery could not be attributed to either.
\item Condition~4 added two smoothing methods at once, so their effects could not be separated, and the bench had used
      only two policies and one task.
\end{itemize}
Setup~v2 therefore compares \drtc{} with a \emph{patient} upstream that has the same limits (Section~\ref{sec:baseline}),
adds the ablations that take \drtc{} apart, tests each smoothing method on its own before combining them, runs the bench
on both tasks and seven policies, and moves the faults by a seeded shift so they also hit the grasp.
