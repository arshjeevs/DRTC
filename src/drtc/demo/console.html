What is real here

The client and the policy are real: the actual LeRobot and DRTC scheduling code runs against SmolVLA on this machine's GPU. The network delay of 250 ms per reply and the faults are injected in the client's transport, as on the bench. The robot is a replayed sorting episode rather than live hardware.
